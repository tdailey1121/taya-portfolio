Step 01 · Source

Where the data came from

Student, enrollment, engagement and advising data that I generated myself, because real student records are private.

studentsenrollmentengagementadvising
Step 02 · What was messy

What I had to deal with

  • Real student data can’t be shared, so there was nothing public to start from.
  • Academic, advising and engagement data usually live in separate systems.
  • There was no single student-by-term view to measure risk over time.
Step 03 · Applied steps

How I cleaned and modeled it

  • Generated privacy-safe synthetic datasets in Python.
  • Modeled relational tables and built a student-term analytics mart in SQLite.
  • Calculated GPA, completion ratios, retention indicators and composite risk scores.
  • Built early alert logic from academic, engagement and advising risk flags.
  • Designed four pages for leadership, advisors and institutional research.
Step 04 · The question

What the dashboard needed to answer

How can a college identify at-risk students early, understand what drives attrition, and prioritize who to help first?

Step 05 · What it shows

What the data showed

  • Next-term retention declines as composite risk rises.
  • Engagement and advising gaps are the dominant risk drivers.
  • Academic performance alone doesn’t explain attrition.
  • Advisors can filter by term, program and class level, then drill through to a single student’s history.

Step 06 · Data quality & limits

What to keep in mind

  • All data is synthetic, so findings demonstrate the framework rather than describe a real college.
