Credit Card Retention & Churn

A three-page Power BI dashboard for PremierBank, showing which customers are likely to leave, what behaviors signal churn, and where to focus retention.

Step 01 · Source

Where the data came from

A single credit card customer dataset with demographics, card category, account activity and an attrition flag.

credit card customers
Step 02 · What was messy

What I had to deal with

  • Some Education Level values had spelling and formatting issues.
  • Attrition_Flag values were hard to read on a dashboard.
  • Age, tenure, inactivity, contacts and utilization were raw numbers with no useful groupings.
  • The data is one point in time, with no customer history.
Step 03 · Applied steps

How I cleaned and modeled it

  • Standardized inconsistent category labels in Power Query.
  • Recoded attrition into Active and Attrited.
  • Created Age Group, Tenure Group, Inactivity Band, Contact Frequency and Utilization Band.
  • Wrote DAX for retention rate, attrition rate, utilization, transaction behavior and customer risk.
Step 04 · The question

What the dashboard needed to answer

PremierBank is losing customers and seeing inconsistent card use. Which customer attributes and behaviors are associated with churn?

Step 05 · What it shows

What the data showed

  • Overall retention is about 84%.
  • Platinum card customers have the highest attrition rate of any card category.
  • Customers with more contacts with the bank attrite more often.
  • Customers with no card utilization show the highest attrition risk.
  • About 737 customers were identified as high risk.

Step 06 · What I’d recommend

What I’d tell the business

  • Reach inactive customers before they fully disengage.
  • Watch high-contact customers for service issues.
  • Run targeted retention for Platinum and other high-risk card segments.
  • Encourage use among low- and no-utilization customers.
Step 07 · Data quality & limits

What to keep in mind

  • Attrition_Flag was the main churn indicator: Active means retained, Attrited means closed or lost.
  • Because there’s no history over time, the analysis shows patterns and indicators, not exact causes.
Step 08 · What I’d do next

Where I’d take it next

  • Add predictive churn scoring.
  • Bring in satisfaction or survey data.
  • Develop customer lifetime value metrics.
  • Track retention campaign performance over time.
