Step 01 · Source

Where the data came from

Synthetic customer support ticket data with channel, agent, chatbot use, response and resolution times, and ratings.

support tickets
Step 02 · What was messy

What I had to deal with

  • Support KPIs were tracked in silos, so leadership couldn’t see the full picture.
  • Raw ticket data needed cleaning before resolution times and ratings could be measured.
Step 03 · Applied steps

How I cleaned and modeled it

  • Cleaned and transformed the ticket data in Power Query.
  • Wrote DAX measures for resolution time, response time, percent resolved and customer rating.
  • Balanced high-level KPI cards with detailed views by channel and agent.
  • Added slicers for agent, channel and chatbot use.
Step 04 · The question

What the dashboard needed to answer

How can leadership see resolution speed, customer satisfaction and bottlenecks in one place?

Step 05 · What it shows

What the data showed

  • Phone support resolved tickets 15% faster than email.
  • Agent A had the highest satisfaction score, 4.32 out of 5.
  • Chat had the longest resolution time, 39.56 hours.
Step 06 · Data quality & limits

What to keep in mind

  • The data is synthetic, so the findings demonstrate the reporting rather than a real support team.
