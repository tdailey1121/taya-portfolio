Detroit Shock Fan Segmentation & Revenue

A four-page Power BI report on SQL-modeled fan data: who the fans are, how each segment spends on tickets and merchandise, and how they engage digitally.

Step 01 · Source

Where the data came from

Fan, engagement, ticketing and merchandise data delivered as separate CSV files.

fans.csvengagement.csvtickets.csvmerch.csv
Step 02 · What was messy

What I had to deal with

  • Four disconnected CSV files with no shared reporting view.
  • Some fans appeared in more than one segment, which would double-count them.
  • A notable share of fans fit no segment at all.
Step 03 · Applied steps

How I cleaned and modeled it

  • Ingested the CSVs into SQL.
  • Cleaned and transformed the data with SQL and Power Query.
  • Built fan-level segmentation logic from behavioral and demographic indicators.
  • Wrote DAX measures to de-duplicate fans who appear in multiple segments.
  • Designed four pages for executive, revenue and CRM stakeholders.
Step 04 · The question

What the dashboard needed to answer

Who are our fans, how do they spend, and how do they engage across channels?

Step 05 · What it shows

What the data showed

  • The fan base is driven by Young Adults (18–30) and Game-Day Casuals, and skews toward 18–44.
  • Merch Loyalists spend the most on merchandise per fan despite attending less.
  • Young Adults and Game-Day Casuals bring in the most total ticket revenue; High-Value Superfans have the highest per-fan ticket spend.
  • Most fans attend only 1–3 games a year.
  • Young Adults and Digital-Only fans use the app more, and channel preferences vary by segment.

Step 06 · What I’d recommend

What I’d tell the business

  • Upsell Game-Day Casuals with bundled offers.
  • Use segment-specific channels for CRM outreach.
  • Refine segmentation to classify more fans.
Step 07 · Data quality & limits

What to keep in mind

  • A notable portion of fans remain unclassified, so segment totals don’t cover the whole fan base.
Step 08 · What I’d do next

Where I’d take it next

  • Track campaign responses.
  • Add churn-risk indicators.
  • Connect personalized messaging through CRM and marketing automation platforms.
