Step 01 · Source

Where the data came from

One used car sales dataset with vehicles, manufacturers, locations, sale status, ratings, commission and feedback.

used car sales.csv
Step 02 · What was messy

What I had to deal with

  • A single flat sales file with no structured reporting view.
  • Raw mileage and sales ratings needed bands before they could be compared.
  • No measures existed for conversion, commission or days to sell.
Step 03 · Applied steps

How I cleaned and modeled it

  • Cleaned and transformed the data in Power Query.
  • Segmented vehicles by manufacturer, car type, location, mileage band, sales rating band and sale status.
  • Built 10 measures: total, sold and unsold cars, conversion rate, revenue, average price, rating, total and average commission, and days to sell.
  • Added slicers for manufacturer, location, car type, sales agent and feedback category.
Step 04 · The question

What the dashboard needed to answer

Which manufacturers, vehicle types, agents and locations drive performance, and where is inventory moving slowly?

Step 05 · What it shows

What the data showed

  • Maruti, Hyundai and Toyota had some of the highest sales volume.
  • Hatchbacks and SUVs were among the most frequently sold types.
  • Some locations held more unsold inventory than others.
  • Customer feedback was mostly positive, led by Excellent and Good ratings.
  • Agent performance varied, pointing to best practices worth sharing.

Step 06 · What I’d recommend

What I’d tell the business

  • Focus inventory reduction on locations with the most unsold vehicles.
  • Review slow-moving manufacturers and types for pricing or marketing changes.
  • Share top agents’ practices with the wider team.
Step 07 · Data quality & limits

What to keep in mind

  • Everything comes from one sales dataset, so the dashboard has no outside or regional benchmarks yet.
Step 08 · What I’d do next

Where I’d take it next

  • Add predictive inventory risk scoring.
  • Deepen profitability analysis.
  • Automate refresh pipelines and add regional benchmarking.
  • Add drill-throughs at the vehicle, location and agent level.
